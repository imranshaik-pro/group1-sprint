# Foundation PDF collection

Eight chapter excerpts from OpenStax / Rice University, checked 12 October 2026. These are foundation resources, not NCERT PDFs, APPSC previous questions or complete India/AP-specific chapters. Printed page numbers are retained; the catalogue identifies source PDF page positions.

| Source book | Authors | Selected material | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Principles of Economics 3e | Steven A. Greenlaw, David Shapiro, Daniel MacDonald | Demand/supply, macroeconomics, inflation, money/banking | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 |
| Prealgebra (2017 PDF revision) | Lynn Marecek, MaryAnne Anthony-Smith | Percentages, proportions | CC BY 4.0, as explicitly printed in this source PDF |
| Introduction to Computer Science | OpenStax and contributing authors, Rice University | Introduction, computer systems/hardware | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 |

Sources and source PDF URLs, full checksums, page ranges, author credits and licence URLs are in `catalog.json` and `source-books.json`. Each excerpt includes an original Group1 Sprint attribution page, the publisher copyright/licence page and selected publisher pages. Content pages and their printed attribution are preserved. PDF structure is optimised and illustrations are resampled to 150 dpi for phone storage, without rewriting the educational content. No publisher endorsement is implied.

Noncommercial licences apply to the economics and computing files. Do not sell, monetise, put advertising alongside these files, or use them for AI ingestion/training without the relevant permission. The collection is for noncommercial study. Prealgebra retains the explicitly stated licence of the downloaded 2017 edition rather than assuming later website terms apply retroactively.

NCERT notice review found inconsistent official notices: an older PDF textbook notice prohibits third-party website hosting; an e-content notice permits certain noncommercial distribution. We have not resolved the applicable notice for specific NCERT files, so no NCERT PDFs are mirrored. NCERT entries refer to its official homepage/catalogue only. NDLI and exam providers have their own access/reuse terms.
